# Offerlane: user guide

Full, searchable help lives inside the app: **Help → Offerlane Help** (⌘?). **Help → Take the Tour** replays the short tour. Those answers ship with each version, so they always match the app you have. This page is the short version.

## Install
1. Download **Offerlane-0.3.0.dmg** from [Releases](../../../releases), open it and drag **Offerlane** into **Applications**.
2. The first time only: Control-click Offerlane in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again. On macOS 15 or later, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet.

## First run (about ten minutes)
1. **Sign in**: create a local profile with email and password. Touch ID is optional. Everything stays on this Mac.
2. **Connect an AI**: the easiest is **Sign In with OpenRouter**. Approve Offerlane in your browser and the key comes back by itself. Pick a free model (ids end in ":free"), or a paid one once you add OpenRouter credit. Other options are under **More options**: Ollama runs on your Mac for free, and Google Gemini, Groq and Cloudflare have free keys. You can also use a Claude API key (paid) or your own endpoint. Each option says what it does with your data before you connect. To use a key instead, click **Get a key yourself instead**, open the key page, copy the key, come back and press ⌘V.
3. **Add your résumés**: PDF, Word, text or LaTeX. Add several versions, such as "IC" and "Manager", and Offerlane picks the best one for each role.
4. **Find jobs**: check the titles and companies Offerlane drafted from your résumé, then click **Find Jobs**.

## Everyday use
- **Home**: today's focus, your readiness for the role you want, and this week's numbers.
- **Discover** (⌘F to find jobs): **Approve** roles worth applying to and **Decline** the rest. Declined roles never come back. Your company list is a watchlist that ranks those companies first. It never limits the search.
- **Review**: every change to your résumé, with your original in red and the tailored text in green. Invented numbers are blocked. **Edit**, **Redo with Note…** or approve. Offerlane then fills in the application and stops at Submit, and **you** click Submit.
- **Pipeline**: every application on a board. Replies in your inbox move cards along if you turn on email tracking. **Insights** shows reply rates by résumé version, source and role type. **Offers** compares offers and drafts negotiation notes.
- **Goals**: one-, three- and five-year paths and pivot options, with your readiness for each.
- **Skills**: the skills your target roles ask for, counted from real postings, plus a learning plan built only from verified links. A certificate goes on your résumé only after you mark it done.
- **Interview Prep**: questions for the role, a STAR story bank built only from your résumé, and a mock interviewer.
- **Résumés**: your versions, a résumé health and ATS check per role, and LinkedIn and portfolio tips.
- **Connect AI**: add backup AIs that take over when the first one is busy or down. The **Setup Center** checks your AI, internet, storage and Mac, and **Repair Everything** fixes problems in one click.

## Something wrong?
Open **Help → Offerlane Help** and search for it, or use **Help → Send Diagnostics…** to create a report with personal details removed. You see the full report before you share it.
