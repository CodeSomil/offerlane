# Offerlane terms of use

*Draft · 11 November 2026 · applies to Offerlane 0.3.0 and later for macOS*

## 1. What Offerlane is
Offerlane is a desktop app that helps you find roles, tailor your resume, fill in job applications and track replies.
It runs on your Mac. It has no server or account system of its own (see the Privacy Policy, `PRIVACY.md`).

## 2. You stay in control
- Offerlane never submits an application on its own. You review each one and click to submit.
- Tailored resumes are drafts. Offerlane rejects tailoring that adds numbers or facts that aren't in your own resume,
  but you are responsible for checking that everything you send is true.
- You are responsible for the accounts you create on career sites and for following each site's own terms.

## 3. Third-party services you connect
Offerlane works with services you choose: an AI provider (with your own key or account), your email (IMAP or Gmail,
read-only), career sites and job boards, and optionally ntfy.sh for phone notifications. Each has its own terms and
privacy policy, and your use of it is between you and that provider. Charges from an AI provider are yours; Offerlane
shows usage and lets you set token limits, but the provider's bill is final.

## 4. Acceptable use
Don't use Offerlane to send false information to employers, to apply in someone else's name, to overload or get around
the limits of a career site or job board, or to break the law or a site's terms. Offerlane keeps request rates low and
honours each job source's terms (`SOURCES.md`); don't change it to do otherwise.

## 5. Your data
Your data is yours and stays on your Mac, apart from what you send to the services in section 3. Export everything,
make an encrypted backup, or delete everything at any time from Settings → Your data. If you lose a backup's passphrase,
nobody can recover that backup.

## 6. No guarantees
Offerlane is provided "as is", without any warranty. We don't promise that it finds every role, that an AI's output is
correct, that a career site accepts an application, or that you get an interview or an offer. Job listings come from
third parties and may be out of date or wrong.

## 7. Limitation of liability

lost data arising from use of Offerlane. *(Cap and wording: for legal review.)*

## 8. Updates and changes
Offerlane checks for updates only when you ask. These terms may change with a new version; the version and date are at
the top. Continuing to use a new version means you accept its terms.
