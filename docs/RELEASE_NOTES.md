# Offerlane 0.3.0

## Highlights
- **Connect any AI in one click:** **Sign in with OpenRouter** needs no key copying. It gives you free models, or paid models from many AI companies once you add OpenRouter credit. You can also connect directly to Ollama (on your Mac), Google Gemini, Groq, Cloudflare Workers AI, a Claude API key or your own endpoint. Each option says what it does with your data before you connect it.
- **Backup AIs:** add more than one AI. If the first is busy or down, the next one takes over.
- **Wider job search:** your company list is now a watchlist that boosts matches instead of limiting them, and searches use more job titles and places.
- **Skill gaps and learning:** see which skills the roles you want ask for, with the postings behind each one, and get a learning plan built only from verified links.
- **Career tools:** goals and career paths, interview prep with a STAR story bank from your real resume, resume health and ATS checks, application insights, networking drafts (you send them), salary and offer comparison, profile tips and market trends.
- **New design:** a calmer layout, a command palette (⌘K) and a review queue.
- **Clearer errors:** no internet, a full disk, a refused key, rate limits and AI outages each show the cause and what to do. A damaged settings file is backed up and reset instead of crashing.
- **Privacy:** Help → "What does each AI do with my data?" covers every AI option.

## Install
Offerlane isn't notarized by Apple yet. The first time you open it, Control-click it in Applications and choose **Open**. On macOS 15 or later, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` if you like.

# Offerlane 0.2.0

## Highlights
- **New look:** a cleaner design with a calmer layout, dark mode throughout, and faster screens.
- **Your profile:**
  - Sign in with email and password or **Continue with Google**, with optional Touch ID unlock.
  - You stay signed in until you sign out.
  - Several people can share a Mac with separate profiles.
  - Existing 0.1 data is offered to your first profile, with a backup made first.
- **Setup in four steps:** sign in → connect AI → add resume → Find Jobs.
- **Setup Center:** installs Claude Code or Ollama for you, checking each download is genuine before it runs. A status dot shows if anything needs fixing, and **Repair Everything** fixes it in one click.
- **Help:**
  - Searchable answers in the app (⌘?) and a short tour.
  - **Send Diagnostics** makes a report with personal details removed, shown to you before you share it.
- **Lower AI costs:** repeat questions are answered from a local cache. A per-run limit and a live usage meter keep spend predictable.
- **Accessibility:** better VoiceOver labels and full keyboard use. Every colour meets 4.5:1 contrast. Reduced motion is respected.
- **Security:**
  - Saved career-site passwords are only ever typed into their own site.
  - Verification links from email are checked before opening.
  - Offerlane still never presses Submit.
- **Gmail (preview):** read-only, for invited testers only. IMAP stays the default.
