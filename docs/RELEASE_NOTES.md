# Offerlane 0.2.0

## Highlights
- **New look:** a cleaner design with a calmer layout, dark mode throughout, and faster screens.
- **Your profile:**
  - Sign in with email and password or **Continue with Google**, with optional Touch ID unlock.
  - You stay signed in until you sign out.
  - Several people can share a Mac with separate profiles.
  - Existing 0.1 data is offered to your first profile, with a backup made first.
- **Setup in four steps:** sign in → connect AI → add resume → Find Jobs.
- **Setup Center:** installs Claude Code or Ollama for you, checking each download is genuine before it runs. A status dot shows if anything needs fixing, and **Repair Everything** fixes it in one click.
- **Help:**
  - Searchable answers in the app (⌘?) and a short tour.
  - **Send Diagnostics** makes a report with personal details removed, shown to you before you share it.
- **Lower AI costs:** repeat questions are answered from a local cache. A per-run limit and a live usage meter keep spend predictable.
- **Accessibility:** better VoiceOver labels and full keyboard use. Every colour meets 4.5:1 contrast. Reduced motion is respected.
- **Security:**
  - Saved career-site passwords are only ever typed into their own site.
  - Verification links from email are checked before opening.
  - Offerlane still never presses Submit.
- **Gmail (preview):** read-only, for invited testers only. IMAP stays the default.
